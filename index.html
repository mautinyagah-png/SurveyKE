Getting started

A simple path to participation.

SurveyKE is being developed in stages. Account registration is connected to Supabase; the survey participation workflow is still being built.

01

Create an account

Register with your email address and choose a secure password. Confirm your email if required.

02

Explore survey opportunities

When surveys are launched, eligible participants will be able to review the available topics and requirements.

03

Share your experience

Participate in available surveys and review any participation terms before submitting responses.

Our vision

Better decisions begin with listening.

SurveyKE aims to make it easier to share real experiences about services and everyday life in Kenya. Our goal is to build a useful, accessible platform where people's opinions can contribute to better understanding and informed decisions.
